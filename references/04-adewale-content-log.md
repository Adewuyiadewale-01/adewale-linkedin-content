# Adewale Content and Performance Log

> Canonical record of published posts, experiments, outcomes, and lessons.

**Created:** 2 September 2026

## Logging Rules

- Add one entry for every published post worth evaluating.
- Record the intended audience, subject area, post format, and goal before or at publication.
- Capture quantitative metrics at consistent review intervals once those intervals are selected.
- Record qualitative outcomes such as thoughtful comments, profile views, connection requests, DMs, leads, interviews, collaborations, and client conversations.
- Do not judge a post only by impressions.
- Separate observation from inference.
- Do not create a permanent strategy rule from one post.
- When several entries support the same lesson, propose an update to Strategy, Voice Guide, or Content System for Tony's approval.
