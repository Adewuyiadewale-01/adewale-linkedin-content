# Adewale LinkedIn Strategy

> Canonical source for positioning, audience, objectives, and strategic decisions.

**Last updated:** 2 September 2026

## Purpose

Build Tony's professional reputation around backend systems, operations, and workflow automation; demonstrate genuine technical competence through useful content; attract relevant opportunities; and document his growth publicly.

The account should show how Tony identifies problems, reasons through systems, builds solutions, and learns from real work. It should not become a stream of generic AI commentary.

Current overarching commitment: a 500-day publishing journey focused on problems, builds, tutorials, lessons, experiments, and progress.

## Positioning

**Primary positioning:** Tony builds backend systems and operations/workflow automation, especially for recurring business processes.

Tony works across:

- Backend systems and services
- Backend architecture, APIs, and integrations
- Workflow and operations automation
- Automation architecture and process design
- AI agents and agentic workflows
- APIs and webhooks
- Scraping, extraction, and enrichment
- Browser automation
- CRM and database automation
- Data transformation and synchronization
- Document processing and knowledge automation
- Automated reporting
- Internal tools and utilities
- Website development and technical optimization

Core tools include Python, JavaScript/TypeScript, SQL, Bash, Apps Script, Sheets/Excel, REST APIs, webhooks, Git/GitHub, cloud services, scheduled jobs, Airtable, and major AI model providers.

## Strategic Goals

1. Become known for practical backend and automation thinking rather than generic AI commentary.
2. Demonstrate how Tony identifies, reasons through, and solves operational and technical problems.
3. Attract backend and automation clients, collaborators, employers, founders, and relevant professional relationships.
4. Build a credible public body of work while improving technically.
5. Create conversations and qualified relationships, not merely collect impressions.
6. Support both authority-building and opportunity generation.

## Intended Audience

The audience is deliberately broader than a developer-only audience:

- Founders and small-business operators with repetitive processes
- Operations professionals and team leads
- SaaS builders and technical founders
- Automation engineers, backend developers, and other builders
- Recruiters or hiring managers seeking backend, automation, or software-engineering talent
- FMP's founders, business decision-makers, and digital-experience audience
- Curious professionals learning how AI and automation apply to real work

Audience priority remains a hypothesis to refine through publishing and results.

## Strategic Territory

The account sits at the intersection of:

- Backend systems
- AI and workflow automation
- Business operations and efficiency
- Websites, products, and digital experiences
- Tony's development as a technical professional

“Systems behind digital experiences” connects FMP and Adewale. Customers see forms, dashboards, bookings, checkouts, and portals; teams experience the data, APIs, approvals, notifications, databases, automations, and error handling behind them.

## Confirmed Decisions

- Adewale is Tony's automation and backend-focused professional brand.
- Practical workflow and operations automation remains central to the positioning.
- Backend systems are an explicit part of the profile, résumé, content, and opportunity strategy.
- The account will document a long-term 500-day building and publishing journey.
- Content must connect technical mechanisms to human, operational, or commercial consequences when relevant.
- The account may serve FMP's audience and a wider technical audience without becoming two unrelated brands.
- Content quality and qualified outcomes matter more than impressions alone.
